    What am I looking at?

    In a helpmate both sides cooperate: Black moves first and helps White mate Black as fast as possible. h#2 means two moves each, ending in mate. The table stores the distance in plies (half moves), so h#2 is 4 plies, and a White-to-move position sits on the half step — h#1.5.

    Alongside the distance it stores how many distinct optimal lines reach mate in exactly that many plies. One line means a sound problem; several mean duals. The counter saturates at 255.

    About this site

    Helpmates, solved exhaustively rather than searched. For each material combination this project precomputes every legal position, storing the distance to mate and how many distinct optimal solutions reach it. Nothing here is an engine evaluation: an answer is either in the table or the position is outside the corpus.

          Helpmate tablebases

          Every legal position in a material combination, solved exhaustively — not searched on demand.

          What a helpmate is

          In an ordinary chess problem the two sides fight. In a helpmate they cooperate: Black moves first and helps White checkmate Black as quickly as possible. Composers publish these as h#n — a position, plus the claim that mate is reachable in exactly n Black moves and n White moves, ideally by a single line of play.

          Because both sides pull in the same direction, solving one is a cooperative shortest-path problem rather than the minimax search a competitive tablebase needs. That difference is what makes it practical to solve entire material classes rather than one position at a time.

          What the tables answer

          dtm
          Distance to mate in plies (half-moves), under optimal cooperation. A Black-to-move position with dtm 2n is h#n; a White-to-move position with dtm 2n+1 sits on the half step, h#n.5.
          count
          How many distinct optimal lines reach mate in exactly that many plies. One means a sound, unique solution; two or more means duals, usually a flaw in a composition. The counter saturates at 255, and the dashboard shows that as 255+.
          themes
          Which composition motifs the solutions exhibit — model mates, switchbacks, excelsior, underpromotion and eighteen others. See the Themes screen for what each one means and what it needs to be decidable.

          There is no evaluation and no heuristic anywhere in an answer. Either the position is in the corpus and the number is exact, or it is outside the corpus and you get nothing.

          The screens

          Explorer
          Set up any position and walk it. Moves are grouped by how good they are for the cooperative goal: those keeping the shortest mate, those that take longer, those that throw it away. The position lives in the address bar, so every link you copy reopens it.
          Puzzles
          Reproduce the optimal line of a composed position, ply by ply, with the tablebase as referee.
          Materials
          The corpus itself: every generated table, its mate-length and solution-count histograms, and how much of it is solvable at all.
          Themes
          The motif registry, read live from the running binary rather than written down here, so it cannot drift from what the detector actually implements.

          Scope and limits

          • Castling is out of scope entirely. No position with castling rights is read or written anywhere in this project.
          • The 50-move rule is ignored — it has no meaning for a cooperative shortest path.
          • En passant is exact but is not part of the index; its effect is folded in when the table is built and when it is probed.
          • Underpromotion is fully searched — promotions to queen, rook, bishop and knight are all considered.
          • The solution counter stops at 255. Above that the true number is unknown, and any theme that needs the full solution set says so rather than guessing.

          How the tables are computed

          Exhaustive fixed-point search over a symmetry-reduced index, verified against three independent implementations.

          1 · Indexing

          Every position in a material class maps to one integer in a dense plane, through a symmetry-reduced mixed-radix scheme. The two kings index jointly, through a precomputed table of non-adjacent king pairs: 462 classes for pawnless material — full eight-fold board symmetry, with the white king confined to the a1–d1–d4 triangle — and 1806 once a pawn is present, since a pawn breaks diagonal symmetry and only the left–right mirror survives.

          Each further piece multiplies the plane by 64, or by 48 for a pawn, which can only stand on ranks 2 through 7. En passant gets no dimension of its own: wherever a double push next to an enemy pawn is reachable, its value is folded in as min(table value, 1 + value of the en-passant capture successor), looked up in whichever smaller table that capture lands in.

          The index bakes in White delivers the mate. Probing a position where Black is the mating side is answered by an automatic colour flip rather than by a second set of tables.

          2 · Generation

          For a material class M, the generator first computes the closure of every sub-class reachable by a capture or a promotion, topologically sorted, because M's own scan needs those tables to resolve its successors.

          Then an init pass marks the illegal cells and assigns dtm = 0 to every Black-to-move checkmate. After that, forward-scan passes run to a fixed point: pass d visits the still-unresolved cells, generates their legal moves, and assigns dtm = d to any cell with a successor already at d − 1. The passes stop when one of them assigns nothing.

          The same step counts the optimal lines: count(p) = Σ count(s) over exactly the successors at d − 1, saturating at 255.

          Every pass parallelises across the index range, in tiles the worker threads take from a shared cursor. Pass d only writes cells that are still unresolved and only reads values strictly below d, so two threads working on different tiles of the same pass cannot race. Multithreaded output is required to be byte-identical to single-threaded output, and that is asserted by the test suite rather than assumed.

          3 · Storage

          A table is four contiguous byte planes over the same index — distance and solution count, each for White to move and for Black to move — so one cell costs four bytes. Files are written atomically and read back through mmap, which is why probing a thirty-gigabyte table costs a page fault rather than a load.

          Three file versions exist. An ordinary table stores those planes raw. A marker table is header and metadata with no payload at all, written when a class turns out to have no solvable cell anywhere. A block-compressed table treats the four planes as one logical byte range, cuts it into 64 KiB blocks, and compresses each one independently with zstd, alongside an offset index and a bounded cache of decompressed blocks in the reader. Random access survives: a probe decompresses at most one block, never the whole file.

          4 · Answering a query

          Because the search already happened, every query is a lookup rather than a search. A probe is one indexed read. An optimal line is reconstructed by greedy descent through the distance planes, applying the same en-passant adjustment on the way down. A scan for compositions walks the plane directly.

          5 · Why you should believe the numbers

          A tablebase is worth nothing if its answers are not trustworthy, so the generator is not allowed to grade its own homework.

          An independent oracle
          A from-scratch cooperative iterative-deepening solver that shares only the move generator with the real generator — no shared indexing, no shared search structure. It re-solves sampled positions from every generated class, checking both the distance and the number of optimal lines, which it enumerates itself. Any mismatch fails the build.
          A third implementation
          Every one of the 368,452 legal KQvk positions is re-solved by a plain breadth-first search written directly against python-chess, with no helpmate code involved in computing the reference values, and checked for agreement on both distance and solution count in both directions.
          Determinism
          Generation must produce identical files on one thread and on many. This is asserted directly, including on a material class where en-passant successors are genuinely reachable — the one code path whose safety depends on values written by another thread.
          Golden compositions
          Known-answer positions are checked exactly, including their full set of optimal lines, not merely their distance.

          6 · What this does not do

          The corpus is finite. A position whose material has not been generated has no answer here, and the honest response is to say so rather than to fall back on a search that would answer a different question. Solution counts above 255 are reported as saturated, not estimated. Theme detection on a saturated position reads only the solutions it could enumerate, and the dashboard says when that happened.

          Privacy

          This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no accounts.

          What this site stores on your device

          Nothing. The dashboard sets no cookies and writes to neither local nor session storage. The position you are looking at lives in the address bar, which is why a link reopens it — that is the URL, not storage, and it never leaves your browser unless you share it.

          There is no analytics script, no tracking pixel, no advertising and no third-party embed. Every stylesheet, script, font and image is served from this origin.

          Cookies set by the network in front of it

          This site is served through Cloudflare, which sets its own cookies for security and bot management — typically __cf_bm, and cf_clearance if you are shown a challenge. These are strictly necessary to deliver the site and carry no advertising or profiling function, which is why you are not asked to consent to them. See Cloudflare's cookie reference.

          What the server logs

          The application writes an ordinary access log line per request: the client IP address, the time, the request path and the response status. No request body is logged, and the API takes no personal data to log — a query is a chess position.

          These logs are kept only as a rolling buffer on the host and are overwritten as new requests arrive; they are not aggregated, not exported and not used to build any profile. Cloudflare separately processes connection metadata as described in its own privacy policy.

          Your rights and who to contact

          Where the access log constitutes personal data, the legal basis is legitimate interest in operating and securing the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and complaint to a supervisory authority.
